# Travel (AFS-FIN-009)

> Sample handbook page for the kb-demo app. Acme Field Services and everything in it are fictional.

Owner: Finance. Last reviewed: March 2026.

## When this applies

This policy covers travel for client work, training and depot visits when the trip needs a
flight, an overnight stay or a hire car. Day trips in your own car or a pool vehicle fall under the
expenses policy (AFS-FIN-004).

## Booking

- Book flights, hotels and hire cars through TripDesk at least 7 days before you leave. A booking
  made inside 7 days needs a reason code, such as an urgent callout.
- A trip costing more than A$2,000 in total needs your regional manager's approval in TripDesk
  before it is booked.
- Fly economy on flights under 5 hours. Premium economy is allowed on longer flights with your
  regional manager's approval.
- Frequent flyer points are yours to keep, but they never decide which airline you book.

## Hotel limits

The nightly limit includes GST but not breakfast, which you claim as a meal allowance.

| Location | Limit per night |
|---|---|
| Sydney | A$289 |
| Melbourne | A$265 |
| Brisbane | A$245 |
| Perth | A$255 |
| Adelaide | A$225 |
| Regional and remote | A$189 |

If no hotel within 30 minutes of the site is under the limit, TripDesk lets you book the cheapest
available room and flags it for Finance. You don't pay the difference.

## Hire cars

- Book a small car by default. Book a mid-size 4WD only when the job sheet says the site is on
  unsealed roads.
- Decline the hire company's extra insurance: Acme's fleet policy already covers hire cars.
- Return the car with a full tank. Refuelling charges from the hire company are not reimbursed.

## Changes and cancellations

Change or cancel in TripDesk as soon as your plans change. A hotel no-show charge is reimbursed
only when the client cancelled the job after 5 pm the day before.

## After the trip

Claim meals, parking and incidentals in ClaimDesk under the expenses policy (AFS-FIN-004), within
its claim window.
