# Laptop replacement (AFS-IT-022)

> Sample handbook page for the kb-demo app. Acme Field Services and everything in it are fictional.

Owner: IT. Last reviewed: April 2026.

## Refresh cycle

Every field technician's laptop is replaced every 42 months. IT emails you a month before your
refresh date with a link to book a swap at your depot.

## Damage, loss and theft

- Report damage within 2 business days on ServiceDesk form HW-7. Include photos, and the job number
  if it happened on a client site.
- Report loss or theft to IT and your supervisor straight away, even out of hours, so IT can lock
  the device remotely. Theft also needs a police report number.
- Your first accidental damage claim is repaired or replaced at no cost to you. A second claim
  within 24 months carries an A$250 excess, deducted from your pay over 4 pay runs.
- There is no excess when the damage comes from a fault, such as a swollen battery, or for theft
  with a police report.

## Loan laptops

While yours is being repaired, collect a loan laptop from the Parramatta depot or have one
couriered to you. A loan lasts up to 10 business days, and IT extends it when parts are delayed.

## Returning the old laptop

- Return the old laptop within 5 business days of receiving its replacement, in the prepaid
  satchel that comes in the box.
- Leave it signed out and charged, with the asset tag still on.
- IT wipes every returned drive before reuse or recycling, so personal files left on it are gone
  for good.

## Getting help

Call the Service Desk on extension 4100, or 02 5550 4100 from outside the network.
