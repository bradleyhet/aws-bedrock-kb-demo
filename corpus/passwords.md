# Passwords and sign-in (AFS-IT-005)

> Sample handbook page for the kb-demo app. Acme Field Services and everything in it are fictional.

Owner: IT Security. Last reviewed: January 2026.

## Your Acme password

- Use a passphrase of at least 16 characters, such as four unrelated words. Length matters more than symbols.
- Passwords don't expire. Change yours straight away if you think someone else has seen it, or if IT asks you to.
- Use your Acme password for Acme only, never for another site or service.
- After 8 failed sign-in attempts, your account locks for 20 minutes.

## Multi-factor authentication

Every Acme sign-in asks for approval in the Acme Authenticator app. If a prompt arrives that you didn't start, deny it and report it to the Service Desk at once, because someone may have your password.

## Password manager

Keep every other work password in Acme Vault. It's installed on your laptop and phone, and it can share a login with your team without anyone seeing the password itself.

## Resetting your password

- Reset it yourself at the self-service portal, which checks who you are through the Authenticator app.
- If you've lost your phone, call the Service Desk on extension 4100. They confirm who you are with two questions from your HR record before resetting it.
- IT will never ask for your password, whether by phone, email or chat.

## Phishing

Use the "Report phish" button in Outlook for any email that asks for your password, payment details or urgent action. If you've typed your password into a suspicious page, change it and call the Service Desk.
