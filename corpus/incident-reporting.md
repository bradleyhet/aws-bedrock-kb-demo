# Incident and near-miss reporting (AFS-WHS-012)

> Sample handbook page for the kb-demo app. Acme Field Services and everything in it are fictional.

Owner: Health and Safety. Last reviewed: February 2026.

## What to report

- **Incident:** anything that injured someone, damaged property or a vehicle, or harmed the
  environment.
- **Near miss:** anything that could have caused an incident but didn't, such as a dropped tool that
  landed beside someone.
- **Hazard:** a condition that could cause one in future, such as a damaged ladder at a client site.

## How quickly

| Event | Tell your supervisor | Lodge it in SafeTrack |
|---|---|---|
| Serious injury or dangerous incident | Straight away, by phone | Within 2 hours |
| Other incident | Straight away | By the end of the shift |
| Near miss | Before you leave site | Within 4 hours of the event |
| Hazard | At the next toolbox talk | Within 7 days |

## Serious injuries and dangerous incidents

1. Make the area safe, then give first aid or call 000.
2. Phone your supervisor, who phones the Safety Manager.
3. Leave the scene as it is, unless you must move something to help someone or prevent more harm.
   The Safety Manager decides whether the regulator must be notified.

## Vehicle incidents

Photograph every vehicle involved, the scene, and the other driver's licence and registration.
Lodge the incident in SafeTrack by the end of the shift and phone the fleet team on 02 5550 4800.

## After you report

Your supervisor reviews every report within 3 business days and tells you what changed as a
result. Reporting is never grounds for discipline: the aim is to fix the cause.
