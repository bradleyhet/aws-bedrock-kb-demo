# Expenses (AFS-FIN-004)

> Sample handbook page for the kb-demo app. Acme Field Services and everything in it are fictional.

Owner: Finance. Last reviewed: February 2026.

## Claiming

- Claim in ClaimDesk within 60 days of spending the money. A claim older than 60 days needs your
  regional manager's approval.
- Attach a receipt for every item. A purchase over A$82.50 needs a tax invoice showing the
  supplier's ABN.
- Claims approved by the Wednesday before payday are paid with that fortnight's pay.

## Meal allowances

When a job keeps you away overnight, claim a daily allowance for each meal you pay for yourself.
No receipt is needed for amounts up to the allowance.

| Meal | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | A$27 |
| Lunch | A$31 |
| Dinner | A$52 |

Alcohol is never reimbursed, even with a meal.

## Using your own car

- Work use of your own car is paid at A$0.79 a kilometre. Log each trip in ClaimDesk with the job
  number and the start and end odometer readings.
- Take a pool vehicle instead when one is free at your depot.
- Tolls and parking on work trips are reimbursed with a receipt or toll statement. Parking and
  speeding fines are not.

## Phone and data

If you use your own phone's hotspot because a client's Wi-Fi blocks the VPN, claim up to A$35 a
month for data.

## Tools and small equipment

Buy tools costing up to A$150 yourself and claim them with a receipt. Anything dearer goes through
the depot store, so it is tagged as Acme property.

## What is not reimbursed

- Alcohol
- Fines and penalties
- Personal entertainment, such as in-room films
- Upgrades you book for yourself
