# Fatigue management (AFS-WHS-007)

> Sample handbook page for the kb-demo app. Acme Field Services and everything in it are fictional.

Owner: Health and Safety. Last reviewed: April 2026.

## Hours limits

- A shift, including travel to and from site, lasts no more than 12 hours.
- Take at least 10 hours off between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
- Work no more than 6 days in a row before a break of at least a full day.
- Callouts count toward these limits, so a 2 am callout pushes back the start of your next shift.

## Driving

- Stop for at least 15 minutes for every 2 hours of driving.
- Drive no more than 600 km in a day, including the drive home.
- Drive between midnight and 5 am only for an emergency callout your supervisor has approved.

## The STOP check

Run the STOP check before each drive and each high-risk task:

- **Sleep:** have you had at least 5 hours of sleep in the last day?
- **Time awake:** have you been awake for more than 16 hours?
- **Other:** are you unwell, or taking medication that causes drowsiness?
- **Plan:** if any answer worries you, stop and call the duty supervisor.

## Calling the duty supervisor

The duty supervisor answers 02 5550 4321 at all hours. They can move the job, arrange a hotel or
send someone else. Nobody is penalised for stopping because of fatigue.

## Going over a limit

If an emergency means you must go over a limit, your supervisor records it in SafeTrack by the end
of the next business day, with the reason and the rest you'll take afterwards.
